Follow-up: `/code-review` (Standards + Spec axes, run independently) both flagged the same defect — `manifest.json` declares `icon.svg` for `purpose: "maskable"` too, and the raw 2a blob's farthest vertex sat ~229px from canvas center vs. the ~205px (40% of 512) safe-zone radius, risking clipping on Android's circular/squircle icon mask. Confirmed by hand-tracing the path. User chose to scale the blob down (not drop `maskable` or accept the clip) — uniformly scaled all path points around the canvas center (256,256) by 0.8508× (after applying the design's own `offsetX:20/offsetY:-25` positioning, before scaling) so the farthest vertex lands at 195px, under the safe radius with margin. Shape proportions and the `TWEAK_DEFAULTS` character (neck/handle/warp/seed/ratio) are unchanged — only overall size shrank. Verified by rendering at 512px and compositing under a circular mask locally; blob no longer clips.
